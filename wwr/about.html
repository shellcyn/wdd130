Summit Rapids Adventures

Here at Summit Rapids Adventures, our mission is to create the most unforgettable experience with white water rafting that brings friends, families and complete strangers together through adventure, excitement and the beauty of nature. We feel that every river has a story, and that every adventure creates memories that will last a lifetime.

History

Summit Rapids Adventures was founded in 1999 by a small group of outdoor enthusiasts who shared a passion for white water rafting and exploration of the outdoors. What started with 2 rafts, a love for adventure, and a dream of sharing that same excitement with friends and family of the river has grown into a company that welcomes adventurers of all experience levels. Over the years, our team of talented and dedicated guides have built it to where it is today creating a safe, exciting and most memorable rafting experiences. From simple peaceful and truly scenic floats to the most thrilling white water rapids you have yet to experience, our goal has and will always be: bringing people together, one river adventure at a time.
